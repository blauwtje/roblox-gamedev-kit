# Owner verdicts, verbatim

Taste memory for `roblox-hardsurface`, `visual-judge` and `blender-hero-asset`.
Read it before the first shape of every run. A verdict that rejects a shape is
binding until the owner reverses it. Lessons with evidence go to
`.claude/lessons.jsonl`, never here.

Format, one block per verdict:

## <asset slug> <version> (<YYYY-MM-DD>) APPROVED | ADJUSTED | REJECTED

> <the owner's words, verbatim>

Means for the next asset: <one line>
