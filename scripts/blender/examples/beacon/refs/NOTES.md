# Reference notes: signal beacon (bright chunky cartoon camp)

No photos are stored here: the kit downloads nothing, so the owner drops
three to six screenshots into this folder and adds one line each below.
Until then these lines carry the intent the SPEC was built from.

- Silhouette to steal: a heavy dark base, a short cream collar, one wide
  glowing lamp head and a dark cap under a carry hoop. The lamp head is the
  widest thing above the base, so the object reads as a light source and not
  as a canister.
- Material break to steal: near-black base and cap, one cream collar, one
  warm accent plate low on the base where a label would go.
- Wear pattern to steal: none. This camp keeps every surface new, so the
  break between parts has to come from colour and from the cut-in grille,
  never from grime.
- What not to steal: cables, gauges and hoses; under 0.3 studs they shimmer
  and vanish at read distance.
