# Reference notes: steel locker (dark co-op horror camp)

No photos are stored here: the kit downloads nothing, so the owner drops
three to six screenshots into this folder and adds one line each below.
Until then these lines carry the intent the SPEC was built from.

- Proportion to steal: a tall narrow box on a heavy plinth, wider than deep,
  doors that read as two slabs with a gap you could slide a hand into.
- Material break to steal: one dull painted body, near-black plinth, cap
  and bolts, rust on the handles, one warm keypad that says locked.
- Wear pattern to steal: paint gone on the door edges and the handle, dirt
  climbing from the floor over the plinth, nothing scattered.
- What not to steal: thin wire mesh and hanging tags; under 0.3 studs they
  shimmer and vanish at read distance.
