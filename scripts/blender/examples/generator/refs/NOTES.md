# Reference notes: twin-drum generator (dark co-op horror camp)

No photos are stored here: the kit downloads nothing, so the owner drops
three to six screenshots into this folder and adds one line each below.
Until then these lines carry the intent the SPEC was built from.

- Silhouette to steal: two equal squat drums on one plate under a carry
  frame (concept C, picked by the owner from four sheets). The twin drums
  are the silhouette; no drum dominates, and that is the intent, not a gap.
- Material break to steal: dull painted drums, near-black plate and exhaust,
  rust frame and caps, one accent panel with one warm indicator.
- Wear pattern to steal: paint worn on the drum rims, soot around the stack,
  dirt climbing from the plate, nothing scattered.
- What not to steal: hoses, cables and gauges; under 0.3 studs they shimmer
  and vanish at read distance.
